FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max):
Torah Learning Tracker helps you stay on top of your daily Torah learning across multiple curricula — all in one app.

Whether you're learning Mishnayos, Talmud Bavli, Chumash, Mishna Berurah, Nach, or Mussar, Torah Learning Tracker keeps everything organized with smart scheduling, progress tracking, and chazara management that works offline and syncs across your devices.

KEY FEATURES

📚 7 Curricula in One Place
Track your progress across Mishnayos, Talmud Bavli, Talmud Yerushalmi, Mishna Berurah, Chumash, Nach, and Mussar — each with full content hierarchies.

📅 Smart Scheduling
Set learning goals with deadlines (Gregorian or Hebrew calendar) and let the app generate your daily schedule. See at a glance whether you're ahead, on pace, or need to catch up.

📊 Progress Tracking
View detailed progress charts, weekly activity breakdowns, and curriculum-by-curriculum completion percentages. Track your learning journey over time.

🔄 Chazara Management
Built-in review stages (Learn, Chazara 1, Chazara 2) ensure you retain what you've learned. The app tracks which items are due for review.

🔥 Streaks & Gamification
Stay motivated with daily streak tracking, points, and achievements. Child mode adds celebratory animations and a reward system managed by parents.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Multi-Learner Support
Create separate profiles for each family member. Parents can monitor progress and manage rewards through a PIN-protected parent mode. Tutors get a read-only view of student progress.

🎯 Multi-Track Learning
Track personal, school, and tutor learning independently — perfect for students juggling multiple schedules.

📶 Offline-First with Cloud Sync
Learn anywhere without an internet connection. Your progress syncs automatically across devices when you're back online.

🔤 Bilingual Support
Full Hebrew and English text display with right-to-left layout support.

PERFECT FOR
• Daily Mishnayos or Daf Yomi learners
• Students tracking school and personal learning
• Parents managing children's Torah education
• Anyone working through a structured Torah curriculum

Torah Learning Tracker is free and open source.
