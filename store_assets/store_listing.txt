Daily Torah study tracker with smart scheduling, chazarah & streak tracking.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max):
Torah Learning Tracker keeps your daily Torah study organised across 9 curricula — all in one app, with intelligent scheduling, chazarah management, and progress tracking that works offline and syncs across your devices.

Whether you're learning Daf Yomi, working through Mishnayos, or studying Chumash with your children, Torah Learning Tracker builds your daily schedule automatically and keeps you on pace to meet your goals.

KEY FEATURES

📚 9 Curricula in One Place
Track your progress across Mishnayos, Talmud Bavli, Talmud Yerushalmi, Mishna Berurah, Chumash, Nach, Tanach, Mishneh Torah, and Mussar — each with full content hierarchies and bilingual Hebrew/English text.

📅 Smart Daily Scheduling
Set a learning goal with a target date and let the app generate your daily task list. It adapts in real time — when you fall behind, it spreads the catch-up across future days rather than piling tasks on one day.

🔄 Chazarah Management
Every item moves through built-in review stages (Learn → Chazarah 1 → Chazarah 2) on a spaced-repetition schedule. The app surfaces what's due today automatically so nothing gets forgotten.

📖 In-App Text Reader
Read mishnayos and Talmud pages directly in the app with Hebrew and English side by side. Mark items complete as you go — no need to switch between apps.

📊 Progress Tracking
View weekly activity charts, per-curriculum completion percentages, and a lifetime knowledge bar across all your learning. See at a glance whether you're ahead, on pace, or need to catch up.

🔥 Streaks & Achievements
Stay motivated with daily streak tracking, points earned per completion, and milestone achievements. A 30-day calendar shows your consistency at a glance.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Multi-Profile Support
Create separate profiles for every family member. Each profile has its own tracks, schedule, and progress — perfect for parents tracking children's learning alongside their own.

🎯 Multi-Track Learning
Track personal, school, and tutor learning independently on the same curriculum. Students juggling multiple schedules can keep each track separate without confusion.

🔒 Parent Mode
A PIN-protected parent mode lets parents monitor progress, manage reward milestones, and configure daily learning goals for children — without exposing the settings to young learners.

📶 Offline-First with Cloud Sync
Learn anywhere without an internet connection. Progress syncs automatically to the cloud when you're back online, and restores instantly when you sign in on a new device.

PERFECT FOR
• Daf Yomi and Mishnayos learners
• Students tracking school and personal learning separately
• Parents managing children's Torah education
• Anyone working towards a siyum or structured curriculum goal
• Families who learn together and want one app for everyone

Torah Learning Tracker is free and open source.
