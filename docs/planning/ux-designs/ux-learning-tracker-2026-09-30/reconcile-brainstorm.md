## Qualitative ideas

- **“As simple as possible”:** dropped as an explicit governing principle (index:extract-subtracks-requirements.md:146; brainstorm.html:1688–1692). The spines specify some simplicity-preserving behavior—optional tracks, no prompt, no empty-state section—but do not name that principle (EXPERIENCE.md:21, 132, 164–165).
- **Family identity:** partly represented as product context and Shabbos behavior: DESIGN names Orthodox Jewish families and a quiet child-facing treatment; EXPERIENCE specifies Shabbos/Yom Tov planned and catch-up views. The brainstorm’s claim that Shabbos is “the thing that shows a frum family built it” is not stated as an experience principle (DESIGN.md:292–295; EXPERIENCE.md:201–213; brainstorm.html:1415–1417).
- **Shabbos genre shift:** behavior is reflected (EXPERIENCE.md:201–212), but “changed genre” / “most distinctive feature” framing is dropped (brainstorm.html:1415–1417).
- **Grey box vs adopted lifetime-tree tri-state:** intentionally changed: lifetime-tree colors/tints and checked/dash/empty checkbox replace the brainstorm grey fill; DESIGN explicitly says grey box is not adopted (DESIGN.md:311–315, 383, 412; brainstorm.html:762–767, 769–834).

- The cascading sketch’s specific grey-box fill, 40% opacity, and example tree (including the one-partial-mishna → grey-seder arrow) are not carried forward; the spines substitute the lifetime-tree palette and 12% state tint (brainstorm.html:762–839; DESIGN.md:311–315, 383).
- The contribution-timeline drawing, chained year bars, hatched “Sep 2028–Jul 2029 · nothing” gap, and its exact question “Finished, or not yet planned?” are dropped with gap detection. The spines specify future starts and Add next year, but no timeline or gap prompt (brainstorm.html:842–911; EXPERIENCE.md:145–147).
- The central “honest / higher / lower” daily-target gauge comparing over-commit and under-commit is dropped. Shortfall remains as a warning card and capacity bar; no gauge is specified (brainstorm.html:914–961; DESIGN.md:385–389).
- The lifetime-event SVG timeline and paired “1 distinct mishna” / “5 learning events” bars are dropped as graphics; event dates, source, repeats, and distinct progress remain as history text and report totals (brainstorm.html:966–1014; EXPERIENCE.md:116–117, 136–137; DESIGN.md:400–401).

This reading will not change until motzei shabbos.”, and “nothing on this screen can be tapped” are not retained verbatim. The spines instead use a generic planned list, “Read only”, frozen status, and disabled controls (brainstorm.html:1212–1234; EXPERIENCE.md:205–210; DESIGN.md:394–396).
