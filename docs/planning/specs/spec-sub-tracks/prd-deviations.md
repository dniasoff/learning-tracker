# PRD deviations — spine wins

Where the PRD (`prd-sub-tracks-2026-09-30`) and the spine (`architecture-sub-tracks-2026-09-30`) disagree, build to the spine. Rows marked as Daniel's decisions override both. Bead `learning-tracker-5ul` tracks bringing the PRD (and its wiki page) into line.

| # | PRD says | Spine decides | Effect on build |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Scope is Mishnayos sub-tracks. Learners without multi-source goals "see no change" (§0, §2.2). | `learning_events` replaces `completions`, `learning_ledger` and `streak_events` for every curriculum (AD-49). | App-wide cutover of about 29 production consumers. Every user is affected by rows 3, 5, 6 and 8, which change behaviour. |
| 2 | FR-23: queued writes inside a lock window are "rejected on sync". | Rules never reject queued writes (parent AD-30). The engine ignores events whose `recorded_at` falls inside a lock, using the settings in force at the time (AD-36). | Lock-stamped events are stored but never counted. Tests assert that they are excluded from derived state, not that the sync is rejected. |
| 3 | §4.9: all capture works offline. | Tutor writes are online-only. The tutor UI is disabled offline and shows no optimistic state (AD-53). | Offline capture applies to owner devices only. |
| 4 | FR-6a: a future sub-track contributes to the forecast from its start date. The PRD is silent on when its ground leaves the main track (§8.1 deferred item). | `holdsGround` includes future sub-tracks, so their ground leaves the main track as soon as it is assigned (AD-34). | Ground assigned to a future sub-track is unschedulable at home until that sub-track ends. |
| 5 | Points are not specified. | One `pts_{eventId}` per earning event. An event earns only if it is a main-track event that newly makes a ref learnt or carries a scheduled review stage. No reversal entries are written, and the balance counts only events the engine still counts (AD-50). | Prevents points farming. The points half of parent AD-4 is amended. |
| 6 | FR-23 sets 10-minute margins on either side of the lock, and leaves the existing app behaviour unaddressed. | 10 min / 10 min replaces the existing 18 / 15 for capture locking. The existing fail-open path when there is no location is replaced by a fail-closed one (AD-36). | Existing users see a changed lock. |
| 7 | FR-26: a tutor can do everything the parent can. | That access is authorized by one `can_edit_learning` grant permission, default true on new grants. Existing grants gain it only by an explicit parent action (AD-53). | Existing tutors lose editing until the parent opts them in. This supersedes the parent AD-22 / W3.43 policy that tutors cannot mark live completions. |
| 8 | FR-3: a free tick counts toward the streak when its date is the day being counted. | A `dated` event counts only if `learned_on` equals the civil date of `recorded_at`. A backdated tick never fills a gap (AD-40). | Backdating cannot repair a streak. Only catch-up events inside their window count toward past days. |
| 9 | FR-27: the parent is notified when a tutor changes "the deadline or the main track". | The notification set is `{goal, mainTrack, mainTrackOrder, mainTrackStudyDays}` and is sent by FCM to parent-mode sessions only (AD-39). | Changes to stages, scope and learner settings are recorded in history without a notification. |
| 10 | FR-23 / FR-24 / UJ-4: during the lock the app stays readable, and the planned view is shown without tappable controls. | **UX override (Daniel, 2026-09-30):** during the lock the app is neither readable nor usable, and the existing full-screen `SacredTimeLockOverlay` stays. The planned list appears on erev only, with live controls (`screens.md` #09). | There is no locked-view screen. The spine's frozen evaluation during a lock (AD-35) and its lock-view wording need amending to match. |
| 11 | FR-17: a siyum fires once when a masechta becomes fully learnt. Backfill, correction and void cases were deferred (§8.1). The spine shows a celebration once per device and never retracts it. | **Daniel, 2026-10-01:** backfill and correction both celebrate when the masechta is in the learner's corpus and fully learnt. A void that un-completes the masechta takes the siyum away, and re-completion celebrates again. | The engine's completed-unit set is the only trigger, and the per-device "shown" key is cleared when the unit drops out. The spine's Siyum convention needs amending. Chazara of a complete masechta still never re-fires. |
| 12 | The PRD is written for Mishnayos: rates in "mishnayos per week", ground as masechta, perek or mishna. | **Daniel, 2026-10-01:** sub-tracks are allowed on any curriculum, with rates in that curriculum's own units and ground at any of its levels. The limits apply per curriculum. Sub-tracks are rejected on a curriculum whose main track follows a calendar program (AD-33, AD-45). | Forms, the ground picker and rate labels are generic. Daf Yomi-style learners can't add sub-tracks. |
| 13 | FR-16: the streak is kept by home learning. (Today the app keeps one streak across all curricula.) | **Daniel, 2026-10-01:** the streak is per curriculum. The home screen shows the streak of the curriculum in view, and the alert fires for each curriculum whose streak is at risk (AD-40). | Existing users' single streak splits by curriculum at cutover. |
| 14 | §4.9: all owner capture and editing works offline. | Learning capture stays fully offline. A governed change too large for one Firestore Rules access budget (more than about 10 governed docs, such as a long reorder or an order reset) is online-only through `writeWithChangeLog`. *Reset pace* and stored pace goals are retired, and goals are rejected on calendar-program curricula (AD-38, AD-43, AD-54). | Large reorders need a connection. The *Reset pace* button goes away. |
