# Comparable-apps research digest (2026-09-30)

- No Torah tracker found with multi-source attribution, lifetime per-unit chazara history, or shabbos-aware logging.
- Torah Tracker (iOS): bulk mark + "reset" for chazara — reset erases prior pass. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/torah-tracker/id1477420780
- Kehati: chazara tracking; review cites no undo for mis-taps. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kehati/id1059791961
- DafStream: best backfill — "mark all through date X" + toggle by masechta/perek/daf. https://dafstream.com/about
- MyDaf: ±7-day missed-daf window. https://mydaf.app/
- CLM MishnahTracker: group deadline charts (unverified detail). https://www.chevrahlomdeimishnah.org/mishnahtracker-app/
- Hifz apps (MarkTrack, HifzLink, Maktab): teacher-logged sabaq/sabqi/manzil streams, predicted completion date, parent sign-off. https://www.marktrack.com/
- StoryGraph rereads: each reread a dated entry, backfillable. Loop Habit Tracker: "skip" day neutral to score. Duolingo weekend amulet: planned rest days improved retention (+4% return, −5% streak loss).
- Implications: dated attributed events not overwrites; offer bulk "through date X" backfill + granular edit; easy undo; shabbos as neutral day; predicted finish date. Multi-source split of one goal appears unoccupied — primary differentiator.
