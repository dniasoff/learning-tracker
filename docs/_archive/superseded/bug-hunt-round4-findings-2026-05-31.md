## [high/calendar-engine] Calendar engine timezone conversion bug: local dates treated as UTC in projection
- File: `/home/daniel/repos/learning-tracker/learning_tracker/lib/features/scheduler/domain/services/local_calendar_engine.dart`
- Symptom: Calendar dates are incorrectly projected for users in non-UTC timezones. Tasks may surface on the wrong calendar day, appear overdue when they shouldn't, or fail to appear at all. The bug shifts dates by up to ±12 hours depending on timezone offset.
- Root cause: _parseDateKey() at line 145 creates a local-timezone DateTime from the database's YYYY-MM-DD string: `return DateTime(year, month, day)`. This local DateTime is then passed through the projection code, where _dayOnly() at overdue_schedule.dart:185 extracts its year/month/day components and creates a UTC DateTime: `DateTime.utc(dt.year, dt.month, dt.day)`. This treats a local date as if it were already UTC components, losing the timezone offset. For example, May 31 local time in Israel (UTC+3) starts at May 30 21:00 UTC, but _dayOnly() produces May 31 00:00 UTC, which is May 31 03:00 Israel time—a 3-hour shift that cascades through all date comparisons in programSchedule().
- Reachability: Production-reachable via _buildProjectionTasks() at scheduler_providers.dart:~385. All program-calendar tracks call programCalendarSchedule() which invokes getEntriesForRange() → _parseDateKey() → projection code (_dayOnly()). The bug affects every calendar-driven track (Daf Yomi, Mishna Yomit, etc.) for all users not in UTC timezone.
- Fix: Change _parseDateKey() to return `DateTime.utc(year, month, day)` instead of `DateTime(year, month, day)`. This ensures the returned DateTime is already in UTC midnight form, making it safe to pass directly to projection functions that expect UTC dates per the P5 pattern documented in date_utils.dart. Alternatively, audit all callers of entry.date to ensure they convert from local to UTC before using it in date-comparison logic.
